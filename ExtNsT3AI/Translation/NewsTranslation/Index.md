## Translate a news record

1. Open the **List** module and select the news storage folder.
2. Create the translation of the news record with the TYPO3 localization (the language flag of the record).
3. In the form of the translated record, click **Translate with T3AI** and choose the provider, for example **Deepl**.
4. Check the translated texts and save.

<Note>
Set the news folder up as a news container: edit the folder's page properties, open the **Behaviour** tab, and set **Contains Plugin** to **News**.
</Note>
