Decide which editors may see or change the chatbot and the conversations.

1. Go to **AI Universe → AI Foundation → AI Permissions**.
2. Under **TYPO3 Backend Usergroups**, select a group (or click **Add Backend Usergroup**).
3. In the **Permission Matrix**, find the **AI Chatbot/Search** entries:
   - **Chatbot Configuration** – view or edit the chatbot settings
   - **Chat Sessions** – view or manage chats
   - **Chat Messages** – read, delete or moderate conversations
   - **Chat Visitor History** – view or delete visitor history
4. Click **Save**.

![AI Permissions with the tabs Groups & Permissions and Permission Matrix](../images/ai-permissions.webp)

Administrators always have full access. More: [AI Permissions](/en/latest/ExtNsT3AF/Configuration/AIPermissions/Index).
