<a id="ask-ai-agent-conversations"></a>

Conversations are stored per backend user. Other users cannot see them.

- Click **New conversation** in the header to start fresh. The previous conversation remains in the list.
- Open the conversation list to switch, rename, or delete conversations. Filter by **This page** or **All pages**.
- The AI provider is fixed after the first answer. To use another provider, start a new conversation.
- Conversations without activity are deleted after the retention period (default: 90 days).

Administrators can change the retention period, the conversation limits, and which conversation opens automatically. See [AI agent settings](/en/latest/ExtNsT3AF/AskAiAgent/FeatureSettings/Index).
